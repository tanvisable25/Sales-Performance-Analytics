
📊 Sales Analysis Project
🚀 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing multi-channel sales data to uncover revenue trends, profitability insights, customer behavior, and regional performance.

The goal is to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights that support strategic decision-making.

🎯 Business Objective

The company operates across multiple sales channels and regions.

This analysis aims to answer:

What is the total revenue and profit?

Which channel generates the highest sales?

Which region performs best?

Are there seasonal sales patterns?

Which products contribute the most revenue?

Who are the top customers?

Is revenue concentrated among a few customers?

📁 Dataset Information

The dataset contains sales transactions with the following fields: Order Number, Order Date, Customer ID, Sales Channel (Wholesale / Distributor / Export), Warehouse Code, Delivery Region, Product ID, Order Quantity, Unit Price, Line Total (Revenue), Total Unit Cost

Time Period: 2014–2018
Records: 60,000+ transactions

🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook, Excel (Data Validation)

📈 Key Insights

Wholesale channel contributed the highest revenue share

Certain regions significantly outperformed others

Revenue spikes observed in mid-year and Q4

Top customers contributed a large portion of revenue

Some products had lower margins affecting profitability

💡 Business Recommendations

Focus on expanding high-performing regions

Improve pricing strategy for low-margin products

Diversify customer base to reduce revenue dependency

Promote high-margin sales channels

Plan marketing campaigns during peak seasonal months
